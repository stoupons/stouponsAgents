# Installation Standards

## What does the home need before a water softener installation?
The installation site needs: access to an existing softener loop or the main water line; a drain within about 20 feet for the regeneration discharge; a standard 110V electrical outlet within reach of the control valve; a level, solid floor; and protection from freezing temperatures and direct sunlight.

## Is a bypass valve installed?
Yes. Every Stoupons water softener installation includes a bypass valve, so water can be routed around the softener for maintenance or in an emergency without shutting off water to the home.

## What photos are required to complete an installation?
The specialist must upload four completion photos: the installed unit showing the bypass valve; the drain line with its air gap; the control valve screen showing the programmed settings; and the serial number label. A job cannot be marked complete until all required photos are uploaded.

## What happens at the end of an installation?
The specialist walks the customer through how the system works, how to use the bypass valve, and how to add salt. The customer then signs to confirm the installation is complete.
