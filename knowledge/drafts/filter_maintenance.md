# Filter Replacement & Maintenance Guide

## How often should RO filters be replaced?
For the Stoupons 5-stage RO system: replace the sediment filter and the two carbon pre-filters every 6-12 months; replace the RO membrane every 2-3 years; and replace the final carbon polishing filter every 12 months. Homes with poor incoming water quality may need more frequent changes.

## What maintenance does a water softener need?
Keep the brine tank supplied with salt. Clean out the brine tank about once a year. The resin inside the softener usually lasts 10 years or more. If the salt forms a hard crust (a "salt bridge") and the water stops feeling soft, gently break the crust with a broom handle or contact support.

## How do I schedule a filter replacement or maintenance visit?
Ask Stoupons to schedule a maintenance visit. Stoupons tracks your installed system and sends reminders when filters are due, and a specialist can replace the filters during a scheduled visit.
