# Stoupons Warranty Policy

## What does the warranty cover?
Water softener mineral and brine tanks are covered for 10 years against manufacturing defects. The water softener control valve is covered for 5 years. Reverse osmosis system parts are covered for 1 year, excluding replaceable filters. Installation labor performed by a Stoupons specialist is covered for 1 year from the installation date.

## What is not covered by the warranty?
The warranty does not cover damage caused by freezing, flooding, fire or other acts of nature; misuse or neglect; failure to replace filters or add salt as recommended; repairs or modifications made by anyone other than a Stoupons specialist; or replaceable filters and consumables such as salt.

## Do I need to register my warranty?
Warranty coverage is activated automatically when a Stoupons specialist completes the installation and the job passes quality review. Keep your installation confirmation; it includes the serial number of your system.

## How do I make a warranty claim?
Contact Stoupons support with your name, address, the serial number of the system, a description of the problem, and photos if possible. Support will open a ticket and, when needed, schedule a specialist visit to inspect the system.

## Who decides if a claim is denied?
Warranty claims are never denied automatically. If a claim may not be covered, it is reviewed by a Stoupons team member, who will explain the decision.
