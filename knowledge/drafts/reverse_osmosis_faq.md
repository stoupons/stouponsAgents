# Reverse Osmosis (RO) Drinking Water FAQ

## What is a reverse osmosis system?
A reverse osmosis (RO) system is a drinking water filter that pushes water through a very fine membrane. It reduces total dissolved solids (TDS) and many contaminants, including sodium, and improves taste and odor. The Stoupons RO system uses 5 stages: a sediment filter, two carbon filters, the RO membrane, and a final carbon polishing filter.

## Where is the RO system installed?
The RO system is installed under the kitchen sink with its own dedicated drinking water faucet. It needs space under the sink for a small storage tank and the filter housings, plus a connection to the cold water line and the sink drain.

## Does an RO system waste water?
Yes, RO systems send some water to the drain while filtering; this "reject water" carries away the contaminants the membrane removed. The amount depends on water pressure and the system design.

## Should I have a water softener and an RO system together?
Many homes use both. The softener protects the whole house from hardness scale, and the RO system provides high-quality drinking water at the kitchen sink. Soft water entering the RO system also helps the RO membrane last longer.
